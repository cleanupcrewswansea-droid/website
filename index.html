About Clean Up Crew

Bigger and better than ever

Clean Up Crew started as a waste removal outfit and has grown into a one-stop solution for property, garden, waste and outdoor jobs across Swansea and the surrounding areas. Same hardworking crew, same honest prices — just more of what our customers keep asking for.

Every job is quoted up front with no hidden fees, and every member of the crew is friendly, hardworking and fully insured. If it needs clearing, cutting, trimming or hauling away, we've got the van and the team to do it.
